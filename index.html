About Me

View my CV

First of all, thank you very much for viewing my portfolio. I spent a lot of time carefully crafting every element of this "online CV", and I am deeply grateful for the attention you may give to this little project.

Currently a third-year engineering student at EIDD in Paris and just about to obtain my degree in Embedded Systems, I have been coding since 2017. It is only recently that I started creating small, concrete projects instead of just coding for my classes.
I find this exercise very rewarding because I can truly showcase what I want to create. Whether it’s small projects for myself or for my beloved ones, or tools designed to help people with specific tasks, I try to work on various small projects on a regular basis.

Once again, thank you for the attention you give to this online CV. I have also placed my CV below my photo in case you would like to download it and see a more traditional version that includes my professional experiences, interests, academic background, and technical skills.

Matlab

As part of my Automatic Control course, I had to use a language designed for control engineers: Matlab.
It is a language that allows for graphical simulations of complex equations to calculate positions and physical errors, using graphs and matrices to set up and control in-game systems.
This can range from simulating a simple bank or stock placement to calculating the orbital insertion of launch vehicles and satellites.

Real-Time Systems

As part of my Real-Time Systems course, I used the C language as well as another language suited for real-time applications: Heptagon.
Heptagon is a language inspired by Lustre, which is quite popular for real-time applications. It’s a rather unusual language in terms of both syntax and functionality.
All the practical assignments involved achieving 3 objectives. They helped me better understand how compilation works and how to compile everything properly, which was probably the most important part of the course.
